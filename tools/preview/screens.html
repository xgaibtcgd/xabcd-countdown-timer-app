Morning Mission · v0.7

Every screen, drawn the way the app draws it

The layout here is not a mockup. Every rectangle is exported straight from the app's own layout solver, so each element sits exactly where the built APK puts it — including on the tall phone that the previous version got wrong. The icons and palettes come from the same source files the app compiles, and the scenery is the artwork that shipped with the project, with the light shafts, bubbles and drifting petals drawn over it. Pick a buddy or a device shape and the whole set re-solves.

Buddy Screen

Turn on Layout bands to see how the solver divided the screen. Those outlines are the real solved rectangles, and they are the same ones the touch handler uses — which is the fix for the old build, where drawing and tapping kept separate copies of every coordinate and had quietly drifted apart.

Switch to Older 16:9 and back to Tall phone to watch the bands take up the slack. The previous version had every position hardcoded for 1080×1920, so on a modern phone the middle simply did not stretch.

Layout from tools/ExportScreens.java, geometry from tools/ExportArt.java, both run against the app's own classes. Backgrounds are the project's own bg_*.png, shown here at half resolution. The drawing is a faithful port of Clay.java, Scene.java and the Screen classes — close, but not the app running. Build the APK to see the real thing.
